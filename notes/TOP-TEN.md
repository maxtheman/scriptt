# Ten questions about one word, and the gestures each takes on board 5

Written before tuning the page (brief, job 12). One word = any word of the message. A "click" is a click or a key; a drag counts
as one.

| # | Question | Where the answer is | Gestures |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | What is this word tagged as? | the link's label above the words (its kind), or its dot when the label does not fit | 0 (1 click when only a dot shows) |
| 2 | How sure was d1, and what else did it consider? | click the link: `d1 · kind`, every option with its probability, `+N` for the rest | 1 (2 for all options) |
| 3 | What exactly was sent to d1? | `▸ state` under the d1 bars: the state and the instructions | 2 |
| 4 | What is that type: its kind, members, alternatives, brand chain, docstring? | the link's page: `T : *`, `A | B`, members, `⊂` chain, docstring (from d1's options) | 1 |
| 5 | Which functions take it, which return it? | the same page: `→ ƒ` and `ƒ →` | 1 |
| 6 | Which request is this word part of, and which place does it fill? | the bracket under the phrase and the function span after it; click a place: its type, value, decision | 0 / 1 |
| 7 | Can I tag it as something else? | drag a type, a constructed type (`Later[T]`), a function or a d1 option onto it; or click a d1 option | 1 drag (+1 to accept) |
| 8 | Can the tag cover more or fewer words? | drag the open link's end/start handle, or `]` `[` `}` `{` | 1 click + 1 drag (+1 to accept) |
| 9 | Can this word fill an open or rejected place? | drag the link onto the place (in view, or in the rail that appears while it is held) | 1 drag (+1 to accept) |
| 10 | Can I take that back? | `✕ esc` before accepting; `↶ z` after | 1 |
